- Classifies regime:
  * Spot BELOW flip = NEGATIVE_AMP (dealers amplify moves, big swings)

WHY THIS MATTERS FOR YOUR OPTIONS:

- Long calls in POSITIVE GEX regime = your calls die, IV crushes, you bleed theta
- The flip strike acts as actual mechanical support/resistance
