### BIGGEST FLAW

The VOL>OI signal is scoring a specific strike and routing the trade to a different strike with no linkage check. In SNDK today, the scored strike ($1,420) and the traded strike ($1,810) differ by $390 — 21% of spot. The institution generating the flow signal may have been positioning for a move to $1,420; the system is buying a put 27% further OTM than that. The signal and the instrument are disconnected at the point of entry.

**Concrete, testable fix:** Before scoring VOL>OI, require that the strike generating the ratio is within 5% of spot, and that the ticket strike selected for the trade is the same strike that generated the VOL>OI signal (or the closest available expiry-matched contract). If the highest-volume strike differs from the selected ticket strike by more than two standard strikes, suppress the VOL>OI score contribution to zero and log a STRIKE_MISMATCH flag. Back-test win rate on VOL>OI picks where the scored strike matches the traded strike versus where it does not.
