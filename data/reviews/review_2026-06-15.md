### PATTERN AUDIT

**VOL > OI — DEGRADED TODAY.** In three of five picks (TSLA, CRM, META), the high-ratio strike is not the strike being traded. The signal is anchoring to whichever strike happened to spike, then the system trades a different strike and inherits the badge without the underlying print. This is a cross-strike contamination problem. When VOL > OI fires on strike X and the ticket is strike Y, the signal value is near zero.

**ABOVE-ASK URGENCY — MIXED.** SNDK at 78% with $18M executed is a real signal. TSLA at 54% and CRM absent are noise. META at 49% is below-threshold and should not score at all. The signal is predictive when percentage is high AND dollar notional is large; it is noise when either is modest.

**REGIME MULTIPLIER (x1.15) — INFLATING JUNK.** Every single pick today had its score lifted by the regime multiplier. META went 35 → 40. AAPL went 54 → 62. Neither deserves the tier it reached without the boost. The multiplier is functioning as a thumb on the scale that promotes weak puts into the scan output on any RISK_OFF day, regardless of whether the actual name's flow supports a put.

**CALL CONCENTRATION AS CONTRA-SIGNAL — IGNORED.** TSLA 77% calls, AAPL 72% calls, META 71% calls. The system logs this as INFO in the contrarian block and does not penalize the put score. That is backwards — when 70%+ of the institutional dollar volume in a name is on the other side of the thesis, that should be a score deduction, not an informational footnote.

**DECAYING FLOW — CORRECTLY FLAGGED, INCORRECTLY TREATED.** SNDK and META both show decaying scores. The system penalizes -15 for decay, which is appropriate, but still surfaces both names as picks. If the score after the decay penalty is below a minimum threshold, the name should be suppressed entirely. A decaying MODERATE is not a pick.

**FRESH FLOW (+10) — NOISE.** CRM's +10 for first scan appearance contributed to it reaching STRONG. Novelty is not conviction. New appearance can equally mean a one-off print that will not repeat. This bonus should be removed or replaced with a confirmation-required filter: score it only after a second scan appearance.

Today every put pick is opposed by: a market tide of +$31.4B to the call side, call premium concentration of 55-77% in each individual name, and NOPE bullish where disclosed. The x1.15 regime boost is converting scores that would not qualify into picks that do qualify, producing a slate of five puts on a day when every measurable flow metric in those names points bullish. If calls dominate the name's own flow, the multiplier does not apply. Back-test threshold against resolved PnL — the resolved-today data already provides the sample.
