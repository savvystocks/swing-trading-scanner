### PATTERN AUDIT

**Signals showing predictive value:**
- **VOL > OI when signal strike matches ticket strike**: INTC today (both $115) is the only clean example. Where signal and ticket are aligned, the new-positioning thesis is coherent. When they diverge by 15%+ (MRVL, QCOM, IREN, LLY), the pattern is labelling flow that does not directly support the specific position.
- **MULTI-SOURCE CONVICTION**: The 4-5 source names (TSLA, MU, NVDA, MRVL) are higher-quality mechanically. The 1-source names (GOOGL, CVNA, AAPL, LLY) have generated the weakest setups. The source count is a legitimate quality filter that the tiering is not fully respecting.
- **SWEEP-THEN-FLOOR (CVNA)**: When size is adequate, this remains a structurally informative pattern. The issue is minimum-size enforcement.

**Signals behaving as noise:**
- **ABOVE-ASK URGENCY on the scored side when the unscored opposing side also fires above-ask**: TSLA, LLY, NVDA, IREN, INTC all have this. The system scores one side and suppresses the other. When both sides are urgent, the signal is directionally meaningless - it indicates high activity, not directional conviction.
- **FRESH FLOW +10 bonus**: This is pure recency bias. It is inflating picks that have zero proof of persistence (MSTR, AAPL, GOOGL, QCOM, LLY all rely on it for material score). Every resolved loss today (AMZN, CRWD, AAPL) and nearly every open loser appeared fresh at entry. Fresh flow with no other corroboration is not a pick.
- **NOPE on individual high-beta names in RISK_OFF**: The TSLA +1.55 NOPE is extreme but the position is already -28% on the prior entry. In a risk-off macro regime, dealer delta pressure from retail call buying can mean-revert violently. NOPE needs a regime filter.
