## D2 — the pond study: **NOT POISONED** (universe exonerated on the arrival test)

unflagged baseline (no external matching confounds). Liquidity-floored, market-adjusted, winsorized.

- **median RV_before / RV_base = 0.843** — flow-flagged names' realized vol was BELOW their own

- median RV_after / RV_before = 0.996 — no vol mean-reversion after the flag either.
- **Consequence for the record: the nine nulls stand as genuine absence of signal**, not as an
  artifact of fishing in stripped water. The universe is exonerated; the flow simply doesn't
