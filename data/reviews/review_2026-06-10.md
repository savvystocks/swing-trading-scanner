**NOPE (scored):** Predictive. TSLA's -1.59 fired and the position gained 44.9% the next day. This is the single most accurate signal in today's data. Keep it weighted heavily.

**VOL > OI:** Predictive above 10x on OI > 1,000. TSLA 72.7x on 3,764 = real. AMD 11.3x on 1,356 = borderline real. GS 3.5x on 350 = noise. The signal needs a floor on both the ratio (min 8-10x) and the absolute OI (min 500-1,000 contracts) to filter single-print artifacts.

**MULTI-SOURCE CONVICTION:** Predictive. TSLA's 5-source appearance vs GS and SNDK's single source is a meaningful filter. Correctly differentiates tiers.

**REGIME TAILWIND x1.15:** Noise promoter. It is taking sub-threshold picks (GS 47, TSM 26) across the pick boundary. It should amplify existing real signals, not substitute for them.

**DECAYING flag:** The system surfaces and then partially scores down a decaying signal but still logs it as a pick. If flow is cooling across three scans it is not a pick. The flag should disqualify, not discount.

**FRESH FLOW bonus (+10):** In isolation, first appearance with no other strong signals is not a positive. GS and TSM benefit from this bonus despite having no VOL>OI and sub-50% above-ask. Remove or require a corroborating scored pattern.

The regime multiplier x1.15 is functioning as a score top-up that manufactures picks out of marginal flow. GS scored 47 raw; TSM scored 26 raw. Neither has multiple fired patterns. Without the regime boost neither is a pick. The result today is two MODERATE picks (GS, TSM) with one real signal each and no corroborating evidence. Meanwhile, the 06-08 CALL book (NVDA, COIN, GOOGL, TSLA) was logged in conditions that appear to have been directionally wrong - suggesting the same regime signal was not applied as a filter on the entry side when it should have been.

**Concrete testable fix:** Gate MODERATE and above picks behind a minimum of two independently scored patterns (excluding INFO tags, MARKET TIDE, FRESH FLOW, and REGIME TAILWIND). Apply this retrospectively to today: GS fails (only VOL>OI and above-ask, one of which is weak). TSM fails (only above-ask). SNDK fails (decaying VOL>OI disqualified, leaving only above-ask and institutional size). Three bad picks eliminated with one rule.
