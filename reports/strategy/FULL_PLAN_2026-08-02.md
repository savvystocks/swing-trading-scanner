## TIER 1 — Levered Premium Engine (premium-lane, $100k)

WHY IT WORKS — the causal chain, step by step:
1. Investors systematically overpay for index downside insurance (variance risk premium —
   Carr-Wu RFS 2009; 30y of CBOE index records; our own 07-28 check: IV > realized on 79% of
   866 ticker-days). The seller of that insurance collects the overpayment as structural yield.
2. Short tenor (1-5 DTE) harvests that premium ~150x/year instead of 12x — more independent
   collections, less calendar exposure per position, faster theta decay per day held.
3. Far OTM (~5%, 2-10 delta) means ~93-97% of contracts expire worthless — the index must fall
   ~5% in under a week to touch us; that is a ~1-3% frequency event historically.
4. VIX-rank sizing sells MORE when premium is rich and stands down when it is thin — the one
   conditioning the academic literature supports (documented +improvement in the arXiv paper
   and Pyun JFE).
5. Modest leverage (1.25-2x) multiplies a high-Sharpe stream — CBOE's own study: +4-6%/yr at
   just 125% notional. ERN proved 2-2.5x survivable for 13 real years including three crashes.
6. Hold-to-expiry avoids paying the bid-ask twice and beat managed exits on Sharpe (spintwig).
PROJECTIONS ($100k): base case +15-20%/yr (+$1,200-1,700/mo avg); good vol year +20-25%; calm
crushed-premium year +8-12%; CRASH YEAR: -10 to -25% drawdown (2020-shaped), recovered
historically within 12-18 months at this family's record. Weekly rhythm: ~46-48 winning weeks
(+0.2-0.5% each), ~4-6 losing weeks (-1 to -6% each). Kill bars per ladder doc.

## TIER 2 — arXiv VIX-rank ATM (put-write, $100k)

WHY IT WORKS — step by step:
1. Same VRP, harvested at the ATM strike where premium density is maximal: an ATM 5-DTE SPX put
   carries ~1.2-1.6% of notional in premium per week (~60-80% annualized gross if fully
   deployed) — the raw material for a 50% target actually exists at this strike/tenor.
2. ATM wins less often (~55-65% of weeks after premium cushion) but the premium collected per
   win is ~10x the far-OTM tier's — the arithmetic shifts from win-rate to premium-mass.
3. The VIX9D 21-day percentile rank deploys size only when that premium is historically rich —
   in the paper this conditioning is the difference between mediocre and 52.77% CAGR
   (documented out-of-sample, 21.6% vol, 9.9% DD, 2024).
4. No stops: crystallizing intraweek whipsaws is what kills ATM sellers; settlement-to-
   settlement accounting rides the noise (per the published methodology and ERN's practice).
5. The 2020-CONDITION makes it survivable: this week's 2012-2026 replication sets the Kelly
   cap so the modeled March-2020 drawdown stays <= 25%. The cap decides the honest target.
PROJECTIONS ($100k): IF replication confirms near published sizing — base +35-50%/yr
(+$2,900-4,200/mo avg), rhythm ~+3-4% avg months with -5 to -10% down months; bear case: a
2020-shaped quarter at capped sizing -15 to -25% (the cap's whole job); crushed-vol year
+10-20% (rank keeps size small). IF replication says the published sizing breaches the halt in
2020, the printed cap lowers the target to ~30-40% — announced at the 08-09 lock, not fudged.

## TIER 3 — Frontier 0DTE MEIC (v8.5 bot, $100k)

WHY IT CAN WORK — step by step (and where it can fail, same steps):
1. 0DTE index options are systematically overpriced (academic consensus incl. JHU/Münster work;
   retail is net LONG these lotteries and loses ~$358k/day — the seller is on the right side).
2. Six timed entries diversify the day: no single entry bears the whole day's path risk; a
   morning trend that kills tranche 1 leaves tranches 4-6 selling the richer post-move premium.
3. The breakeven stop (close a side when its loss = credit received) makes each side's worst
   case ~zero minus slippage — bad days cluster near flat instead of deep red.
4. Defined wings cap the tail: the account CANNOT gap to ruin; worst case per condor is
   width-minus-credit, known at entry.
5. 252 days x small positive edges compound: the live claim is 20.7% CAGR at 4.31% DD; scaled
   community backtests print 100%+ CAGR. The gap between those numbers is FRICTION (24 legs/
   day of spread crossings) and stop granularity — which is precisely what our fill ledger and
   10-minute Phase A measure. This tier's deliverable is that number.
PROJECTIONS ($100k): measurement year base +20-40% (capture 40-60% of backtest); upside if
capture >70%: +60-100%; failure mode: capture <= 0 at 40 fills -> book killed, finding banked.
Daily rhythm: target ~+0.3-0.5% collected, ~65-75% green days, red days -0.5 to -1.5%.
