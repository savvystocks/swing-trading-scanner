**VOL > OI — BROKEN AT LOW OI.** The signal is predictive when base OI is substantial (GOOGL at 11,800, NVDA at 79,216). The signal should be suppressed below a minimum OI threshold of at minimum 500 contracts, and ideally 2,000+.

**ABOVE-ASK URGENCY — USEFUL BUT MISWEIGHTED.** At 55%+ with $20M+ premium on a liquid large-cap (GOOGL), it is a real signal. At 43% (AVGO), 49% (NOK contrarian side), or 50% (TSLA), it is statistical noise. The system is treating any value above 50% as signal-equivalent. It is not. Threshold should be 58%+ and premium-adjusted.

**NOPE — INCONSISTENT QUALITY.** NOPE +1.77 on NEE (utility) is the exact type of mean-reversion-prone extreme that should be flagged rather than rewarded. NOPE +0.40 on GOOGL is modest but in a direction-consistent, liquid name with corroborating signals. The pattern is predictive in mega-cap tech with multiple confirming signals, and noise or contrarian in utilities and thin names. Apply sector cap on NOPE scoring.
