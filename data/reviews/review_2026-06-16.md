**BIGGEST FLAW**

The VOL>OI pattern fires on whichever strike within the ticker has the highest vol/OI ratio - not necessarily the ticket strike being recommended. When OI on a fringe strike is 174 or 419, any modest volume creates a 35x or 270x ratio that looks like institutional conviction but is a divide-by-zero-adjacent artifact. Two of today's four STRONG or lower picks scored on this fake signal.

**Concrete, testable fix:** Gate the VOL>OI pattern so it only scores when the high-ratio strike is within 10% of the ticket strike being recommended. Reject the signal entirely if the scored strike deviates more than 10% from the ticket. Backtest: compare win rate on picks where VOL>OI fires on-ticket vs off-ticket over the last 60 sessions. The hypothesis is the off-ticket cohort will show no edge over base rate.
