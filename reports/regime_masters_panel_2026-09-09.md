# REGIME MASTERS - PANEL VERDICT (2026-09-09)

Method: 4 adversarial lenses + verifying judge, 108 code reads.

## VERDICT
BUILD-WITH-CHANGES — the architecture survives, the drafted mechanism does not. Masters as regime-gated entities that accumulate proven conditions is sound and charter-aligned (NORTH_STAR.md:48, "at most a regime portfolio of 2-4 proven strategies"). But three of the six sections must be REPLACED, not patched: §1's merge vocabulary and clock-restart, §3's calendar cadence cap, and §4's reuse of the auto_* rollback machinery. Step 1 as drafted ("seeded masters, behaviour-identical") is DO-NOT-BUILD — I verified it is provably false: a rename silently drops each anchor's pool, trigger-contract, structure and exit bindings, and `_tuned()` returns None so `sandbox_proactive_lab.py:1145-1149` falls back to stop 50 / trig 50 / give 0.20, whose own comment reads "the -50 stop sat inside bear whipsaw range and cost a third of the measured edge". The one idea in the findings set that rescues the whole design is evidence-lens finding 3's observation, which I verified: AND-only merging means each merged cohort is a strict SUBSET of the pre-merge trade set, so every counterfactual condition-set is already computable over trades the master has recorded. Retroactive re-scoring replaces clock-restart, and the accrual problem collapses from fatal to manageable. Timing helps: §7 already queues this behind proof Phases B/C, so none of this is urgent and nothing needs shipping half-fixed.

## OPEN OWNER DECISIONS

- CONSOLIDATION vs ACCRUAL SPEED — the central trade, and it needs your number, not our intuition. A master is regime-gated, so its day supply is a strict subset of its anchor's, and each AND-merge narrows it further, while the bar wants >=8 days SHARED with the control and then throws away the best and worst (scripts/sunday_boundary.py:352, :364). Masters therefore reach verdicts STRICTLY SLOWER than the probes they replace. What they buy is the charter's own non-goal — 'not a strategy supermarket' (NORTH_STAR.md:48). Options: (a) build masters and accept roughly one verdict per quarter for BULL and multi-month for BEAR; (b) skip masters and instead cull the 8-name probe.priority roster competing for a hard 10-attempt cycle budget (sandbox_proactive_lab.py:2467) — that raises every survivor's fill rate immediately with zero new subsystem and zero clock resets; (c) cull first, masters later. We should publish the measured accrual table for (a) and (b) from origin data before you choose.
- MASTER_BEAR AT ALL — bear regimes were about 13% of the last two years by the spec's own v2.4_note, and reports/shadow_lab/breaker.jsonl records 20 bear days in all of 2026 across 3 episodes. A bear master's verdict is a multi-month instrument arriving in bursts, and by §4's own rule a merge into it restarts everything. Options: (a) build all three masters as drafted; (b) build BULL and MILD, leave bear as the single named DIP_CONVEXITY strategy until bear-day supply justifies a master; (c) build BEAR but exempt it from merges entirely — a frozen bear entity, tuned only.
- THE MERGE UNIT — predicate-only or whole-config bundle. Predicate-only keeps §1's clean claim that merges are data commits and never code edits, but it drops the half of every anchor's edge that lives in pool selection, trigger-contract override and exit shape, which is where fade_book_spec.json's own evidence notes locate it (dip_conf_mild: 'the edge lives in EXPENSIVE trigger contracts... measured ON THE TRIGGER CONTRACT'). Whole-config is honest but the 'data commits, never code edits' safety claim has to be struck and the whitelist grows to cover selectors. A third option: allow predicate-only merges but require any non-representable idea to arrive as its own probe and stay one.
- ACK CEREMONY AND SEATED-MASTER MUTATION. Two calls. (a) Does a merge ACK carry the same weight as the first-promotion owner-ACK gate in reports/proof_account_build_contract_2026-09-06.md:34-35, or a lighter one? (b) When a master holding a proof seat needs a merge rolled back, does that UNSEAT it (our recommendation — it goes back to court for a fresh case with a newly frozen capture denominator) or does the rollback apply mid-stint? §5 freezes merges but says nothing about rollbacks, and rollback is currently the one mutation path left open on a live stint. Either answer is defensible; both spend against the £2,500 lifetime cap, so it is yours.
- THREE LIVE DEFECTS THIS REVIEW EXPOSED THAT MASTERS DID NOT CAUSE — fix now, or fix inside the masters build? (1) scripts/tuner_apply.py:191-192 writes exit glides with no auto_* marker, so the anti-cube clock and frozen_keys at scripts/sunday_boundary.py:264-279 cannot see a Friday glide — verdicts are being earned today against configurations that changed under them. (2) The blanket except at scripts/sunday_boundary.py:246-247 wrapping the whole demotion loop is a single point of failure for the entire ratchet, independent of masters. (3) `-X ours` on every spec push can silently discard a write that the telegram then reports as applied. All three are cheap, all three are load-bearing for masters, and (1) and (3) are quietly costing evidence integrity right now.
