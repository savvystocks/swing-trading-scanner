## Lesson candidates updating from this trade

1. **Picker rule needed:** require expiry to fall at least 7 calendar days BEFORE the next earnings date, not after. The Hunter scorer already penalises near-earnings names; the picker doesn't. Wire this into the contract picker before the next live trade.
2. **Volume/accumulation tagging:** the scanner could compute a 15-day acc/dist count per ticker as part of the Hunter score. Pure-momentum names with 0 distribution days are higher quality than names with the same return but mixed acc/dist.
3. **Sector peer earnings as a leading signal:** when sector heavyweights (HAL, BKR for energy services) beat, the small-cap pure plays in the same group often run ahead of their own prints. This is a repeatable pattern worth scoring.
4. **Position size discipline:** $1,832 vs $1,500 cap was a 22% overrun. Even if this trade works, the cap rule has to hold for trade #2 onwards or risk management means nothing.
