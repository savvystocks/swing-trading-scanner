1. The single biggest reason winners won: the exit machinery, not the entry signal

Every one of the 68 winners was collected by the profit machinery — trailing exits and take-profits. Not one stop-loss or break-even exit ever produced a winner (by construction), and the stop bucket is where all the money died:

The asymmetry is actually good: the average winner (+738$) is bigger than the average loser (-598$), because trails let winners run to +52% average while stops cap losses. The system's fatal number is pure frequency: at this asymmetry it needs 45% winners to break even and the entry signal delivers 16.7%. The exits are a working organ bolted to a broken signal.

2. WHY each trade was executed — the buy triggers, and which of them won

The buy chain: every ~10 minutes the scanner pulls Unusual Whales flow alerts (≥$25k premium, contract $0.30–4.00), scores direction (flow side ±2, 20-day trend ±1, market ±0.5), applies the loose regime gate, and executes the largest-premium surviving alert. This table joins each of the 379 closed legs (through 07-28) to its executed harvest row and asks: which trigger states won? Base win rate of this joined set: 15.3%. "Share of winners" = how many of the 58 winners carried that state.

The headline: the engine's conviction is INVERTED. Its highest-confidence buys — flow, trend, and market all agreeing — won 7.0% of the time (5 winners on 4 days). Its "everything disagrees" buys won 25.6%. Every layer of agreement it stacked made the trade WORSE: with-trend 10.9% vs against-trend 19.9%; with-market 12.9% vs against-market 18.8%; with-tape 13.3% vs contra-tape 17.8%. Bigger flow premium did not help either: the $250k+ "whale" alerts won 13.6% vs 17.0% for the 100–250k band, and the biggest-premium-first rule is what selected every trade. The machine bought conviction at the top and its winners came from buying disagreement.

7. The honesty panel — read before drawing conclusions

  • The engine's own recorded "reasons" are partly fiction. The insider-cluster and reddit blocks are mock placeholders in every record to this day (identical $1,250,000 / +540% values), and the per-trade "determining factor" stories were generated from them. Example from the record: "Insider cluster buy ($1,250,000/10d) predicted the bullish expansion" — written on a trade whose insider data never existed. This table uses only blocks whose source tag shows real data.
  • June-era winners (54% of the money) carry fully mock sensors and 4x sizing — they prove the rally paid, not that any block worked.
  • Day clustering: the best combos span 4–18 distinct days; anything at the low end is one week's mood. The most robust winning condition by day-spread is CALL + below-SMA20 (26.9% wins across 18 days, n=130).
  • Win rate ≠ profit: not a single condition or combination shows positive net P&L at n≥15 beyond noise-level (+$427 best). The finding is "loses slower", never "makes money".
  • This matches the label studies: D4 proved the features contain zero stock-direction information (AUC 0.4955). The winners here won on volatility paths the trails happened to catch — which is why the only consistently profitable organ is an exit rule, not any entry block.
